What this shows. These figures are Cites per Document (2 years) as computed by scijournal.org from SCImago/Scopus data — citations in a given year to the journal's documents from the two preceding years, divided by all documents published in those years. This is not the Clarivate Journal Impact Factor (JIF), which divides by “citable items” only (research articles and reviews) while still counting citations to news, editorials and correspondence in its numerator. The two agree closely for primary-research journals but diverge sharply for journals carrying a lot of front matter — scijournal illustrates this with the BMJ (not in this set), at 2.95 on this metric versus a JIF of 93.6. Comparing all but 8 of the 104 journals here against their published JIF, the median difference is 3.7% and 86% sit within 10%. The widest gaps in this set are Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment (about 54% lower) and Current Biology (about 43% lower) — journals that carry a lot of news, commentary and other non-research content.   • Many journals here are missing a 2023 figure — that year is absent across much of scijournal.org's archive (a gap in the source, not a zero). Where a gap sits between two known years, the line bridges it with a linearly interpolated estimate — shown as a dotted segment and a hollow point, and marked with "~" in the tooltip and table. A gap before a journal's first tracked year or after its most recent one is never interpolated, so the line still breaks there.
  • These are 104 established journals across nine fields — a curated selection, not a ranked "top 100" or an exhaustive census. Field boundaries overlap in reality (many titles could sit in two groups); the grouping is one reasonable organization, not an official taxonomy.
  • Excluded by design: Frontiers Media and MDPI titles, and journals published under the Hindawi imprint, are left out over editorial-integrity concerns. Note that Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment is included — despite the name it is published by Wiley for the Ecological Society of America and is unrelated to Frontiers Media. Aquaculture Research is likewise a long-standing Wiley hybrid journal, not a Hindawi title.
  • Values span a wide range across this set — use "Log scale" whenever comparing journals from different groups so smaller values stay legible.
Different trackers occasionally disagree by a few tenths on the same year; treat these figures as indicative of trend, not a substitute for the official JCR record.
